Hearth & Wheat
Our Story

Built around one wood-fired hearth.

Hearth & Wheat began as a single oven in a converted barn outside the city — built by hand, fed with oak, and never replaced since. Everything we bake still starts there. We believe that good bread requires patience, quality ingredients, and a deep understanding of fermentation.

Head Baker

Maren Holt

"My grandmother taught me to read a sourdough starter the way you'd read weather. We've kept the same one going since this place opened — it knows our hearth better than we do."

What we won't compromise on

Three rules we bake by

Slow fermentation

Every dough gets the time it needs — 24 to 48 hours, never rushed with additives.

Whole ingredients

Flour, water, salt, wild yeast. If a label needs explaining, it doesn't go in the dough.

Baked to sell out

We bake only what we expect to sell each day — nothing frozen, nothing held over.

How we got here

A timeline, not a brand story.

2019

The Hearth is Built

The hearth oven is built by hand in a converted barn outside Cirebon. First loaves sold from a folding table.

2020

Wild Yeast

Our sourdough starter — still in use today — is established from local wild yeast and flour.

2021

Stone Milling

We add a stone mill on-site, moving away from store-bought flour entirely.

2022

Pastry Program

Laminated dough, cinnamon rolls, and our first hand-built pies are introduced to the menu.

2024

First Storefront

We open our first proper storefront, keeping the original hearth oven as the only oven we use.

2026

Still Six Bakers

Still six bakers, one oven, no commercial yeast — and no plans to change that.
